MPS Mobile Account Deletion

If you want to delete your MPS Mobile account and associated data, you may submit an account deletion request using the contact information below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to:

richard.devera100@deped.gov.ph

Request Account Deletion

Please include enough information for us to identify your MPS Mobile account, such as your name, employee ID, school ID, and division.

What Will Be Deleted

Upon verification and processing of a valid deletion request, information associated with the account may be deleted, including:

  • Name
  • Employee ID
  • School ID
  • Division
  • Device identifier
  • Subscription-related information associated with the account
  • Educational and reporting information associated with the account, where applicable

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes.

Subscriptions

Deleting an MPS Mobile account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. If you have an active subscription, you should cancel it through Google Play separately to prevent future subscription charges.

Alternative Deletion Method

Users who still have access to MPS Mobile may also submit an account deletion request through the account deletion feature available within the application.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how MPS Mobile handles personal information and educational data, please review our Privacy Policy.
