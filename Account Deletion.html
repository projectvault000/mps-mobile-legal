MPS Scanner / MPS Mobile Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data without having the app installed.

Send a request

Email richard.devera0092@gmail.com with the subject “MPS Mobile Account Deletion Request”. Include the email used for the account and enough school, division and school-year information to locate it. Do not send your password or student rosters.

We may ask you to verify account ownership. You can also open Profile, Account & data, Delete my account inside the app.

Data covered by the request

The request covers your account, teacher name, account email, school and division profile details, generated device identifier, authentication access, and other personal data associated with the account. An administrator processes the request and confirms the outcome.

Records that may remain

Submitted school assessment records may have to remain for educational reporting, legal or security requirements, with the teacher's identity removed where appropriate. The administrator must tell you which records remain, the reason and the retention period. Deletion of the teacher account must not be represented as deletion of every learner's institutional assessment record.

Local records, exported files and camera photographs on your device are separate copies. Remove them from the app, device files or gallery as applicable. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may permanently remove local app records; it does not submit a backend deletion request.

This version has no in-app subscription to cancel.
