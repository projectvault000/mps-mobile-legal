MPS Mobile Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MPS Mobile collects, uses, stores, protects, and handles information provided by users of the MPS Mobile application and its related services.

By using MPS Mobile, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

MPS Mobile collects information necessary to provide account access, educational reporting, data management, and related application services.

Account Information

When creating an account, users may provide:

  • Name
  • Employee ID
  • School ID
  • Division

Educational and Reporting Information

When users create or submit reports through MPS Mobile, the information may include:

  • Learner Reference Number (LRN)
  • Assessment or academic scores
  • Mean scores
  • Mean Percentage Score (MPS)
  • Number of learners who passed
  • Learning Competency (LC) mastery levels
  • Mastered Learning Competencies
  • Not-mastered Learning Competencies

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through MPS Mobile is used to provide and operate the application's features and services.

This may include:

  • Creating and managing user profiles and access
  • Verifying authorized user access
  • Providing access to authorized application features
  • Processing and generating educational reports
  • Calculating and displaying educational metrics
  • Managing submitted reporting information
  • Providing subscription-enabled features
  • Maintaining and improving application functionality
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the service

3. Data Storage and Backend Services

MPS Mobile uses Supabase as part of its backend infrastructure for application data storage and related backend services.

Information submitted through the application may be transmitted to and stored on backend systems in order to provide the services described in this Privacy Policy.

4. Educational Data and Learner Information

MPS Mobile may process educational information and learner-related information submitted by authorized users for reporting and educational measurement purposes.

Users are responsible for ensuring that information submitted through MPS Mobile is accurate and that they are authorized to submit and process such information.

MPS Mobile does not use learner information for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

5. Subscription Services

MPS Mobile may offer certain features through a monthly subscription. Subscription-enabled features may include report-sending functionality and web-based account access.

Subscription purchases made through the Android application are processed through Google Play's billing system. MPS Mobile does not directly collect or store users' Google Play payment card information.

Subscription status may be processed by the application's backend in order to determine whether a user is authorized to access subscription-enabled features.

6. Information Sharing

MPS Mobile does not sell users' personal information or educational data.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, including backend and infrastructure providers such as Supabase.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by MPS Mobile against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data transmitted between the application and backend services is intended to be protected using appropriate security measures, including encrypted network communications where supported.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain account records, support reporting functionality, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their MPS Mobile account and associated personal information.

To request account or data deletion, contact us using the contact information provided below.

Requests will be reviewed and processed in accordance with applicable requirements and legitimate data-retention obligations.

10. Children's Privacy

MPS Mobile is intended for authorized users who use the application for educational and professional reporting purposes. The application is not specifically directed toward children as its intended users.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, backend services, legal requirements, or data practices.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
