MPS Scanner / MPS Mobile Privacy Policy

Updated November 2, 2026

This policy describes the MPS Scanner Android app, also called MPS Mobile. It is a tool for authorized teachers to scan answer sheets, manage local assessment records, and submit MPS reports. Privacy and account support: richard.devera0092@gmail.com.

Information on your device

The app saves teacher profile details, student rosters, names, Learner Reference Numbers (LRNs), class and section information, answer keys, scanned answers, assessment results, and saved numeracy and literacy configurations in its local app storage. Student names and rosters help teachers identify results on their device.

Camera access is used for answer-sheet photographs and QR scanning when you start those actions. Name recognition is processed on the device. The OCR engine and language files may require an initial download. Camera photographs may be saved in device Pictures storage. Files you export are saved to a location on your device; those copies remain under your control.

Information sent to online services

  • Account creation, sign-in, restoration and account requests send the necessary teacher name, email, school information, school year, region, division, generated device identifier and authentication information to the configured division backend.
  • When you submit an MPS report, the app sends teacher and school details, assessment information, scanned students' LRNs and scores, competency outcomes, and summary and enrollment counts to the division reporting backend. Student names and unscanned roster entries are excluded from that MPS submission payload.
  • Google-hosted public workbooks supply school directories, competencies and assessment configurations. The app uses HTTPS to retrieve them. OCR components and language files are obtained from third-party delivery services. Such providers receive normal connection information, such as an IP address, when contacted.

The division backend uses Supabase infrastructure. Authorized school or division administrators process submitted reports and account requests. Information is used for authentication, educational analysis, reporting, support and service security. NUMERO results in this version remain on the device; the app does not submit them to the consolidation backend.

Purchases, advertising and sharing

This version is free and does not offer an in-app subscription or Google Play purchase flow. The app does not include an advertising SDK and does not sell personal or learner data. Information is processed by the service providers and authorized reporting recipients needed to operate the described features, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

Security and retention

Network requests use HTTPS. Local app data is protected by Android's app sandbox; this is not a promise that every local field has separate encryption. Automatic app backup and transfer are disabled in this release. Backend security and record retention are administered by the responsible division.

Local data remains until removed from the app or app storage is cleared. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may permanently remove local records. Exported files and photographs in shared device storage must be removed separately. Backend account information is kept while needed for the account and service. Submitted school records may be retained for educational reporting or other applicable record-keeping requirements; the responsible administrator must identify any retained categories, reason and retention period when handling a deletion request.

Account and data deletion

Open Profile, Account & data, Delete my account, or use our public account deletion request page. You may also email the support address above without installing the app. We may verify account ownership before processing the request. We will explain any records that must be retained and their retention period. Uninstalling does not delete your backend account.

Learner information and intended users

The app is intended for authorized adult educators, rather than children as account holders. It processes learner information supplied by those educators for educational purposes. Users must have authority to process and submit the learner information they handle.
